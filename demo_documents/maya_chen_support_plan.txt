Participant: Maya Chen
Preferred name: Maya
Plan period: 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027
Prepared by: Harbour Pathways Support Coordination

PARTICIPANT PROFILE
Maya Chen is a 29-year-old participant living in a supported apartment in Northbridge. Maya communicates using speech and a visual choice board when she is tired or overwhelmed. Maya would like to build confidence travelling locally, cooking simple meals, and taking part in community art activities.

GOALS
1. Maya will practise catching the bus to the community art studio with decreasing support, progressing from full support to a visual travel checklist and one verbal prompt.
2. Maya will prepare three simple evening meals each week using a picture-based recipe and safe kitchen routines.
3. Maya will attend one community art session each fortnight and identify one social interaction she enjoyed after each session.

SUPPORT STRATEGY
Support workers will use short instructions, visual schedules, and a five-minute warning before transitions. Travel practice should begin on the quiet Tuesday morning route. The agreed sequence is: check the route, pack the travel card, walk to the stop, identify the bus number, tap on, and check the return time. Staff should record the level of prompting required after each practice.

FUNDING AND SERVICE DETAILS
Core supports are allocated for daily living and community participation. Harbour Pathways will provide up to six hours of support each week. The occupational therapist will review kitchen safety in October 2026. Maya's sister, Evelyn Chen, is the nominated informal support contact for plan reviews.

RISKS AND SAFEGUARDS
Maya can become distressed in crowded or noisy environments. Staff should offer a quiet space, reduce verbal demands, and contact the on-call coordinator if Maya cannot safely continue. No restrictive practice is authorised in this plan.

SUCCESS MEASURES
Progress will be reviewed using travel prompts, completed meal checklists, attendance at art sessions, and Maya's own feedback. The support coordinator will complete a formal review in December 2026.
